HOW I APPROACH A BUILD

From idea to first release.
Then improve with purpose.

Whether it's my own product or a client project, I try to keep the process straightforward: understand the problem, define the smallest useful version, build it, and learn from how it works in practice.

01

Find the problem worth solving

I start by understanding who the product is for, what is frustrating today, and what outcome would actually be useful.

↘
02

Define the first version

I identify the essential features, choose a sensible technical approach, and keep unnecessary complexity out of the first release.

↘
03

Build, test, and ship

I develop the core experience, test the important paths, and prepare a usable version to share with users or the client.

↘
04

Learn and improve

I use feedback and real usage to identify what works, what needs fixing, and which improvements are worth making next.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

Build with intention.

A good product doesn't need to do everything. It needs to solve the right problem and improve over time.

01

Keep it focused

Start with the core problem and avoid adding features that don't make the first version more useful.

02

Learn by shipping

Put a working version in front of people, listen to feedback, and make informed improvements.

03

Stay practical

Choose tools and technical approaches that fit the problem, the budget, and the people who will use the product.
